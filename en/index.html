Who we are

Global technical rigor, local proximity

Chile faces a structural challenge in managing its tailings deposits. According to the National Tailings Deposits Plan for Sustainable Mining, led by the Ministry of Mining, the country has a historic opportunity to turn an environmental liability into a driver of innovation and sustainable development.

In this context, FHI delivers specialized engineering, from geotechnical characterization to real-time monitoring, to safeguard people, the environment and the operational continuity of mining operations. Our team brings together professionals with decades of experience in Chilean large-scale mining and in international projects under GISTM standards. We combine an agile structure with the technical solidity that the most complex projects demand.

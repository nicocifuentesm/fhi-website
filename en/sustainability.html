Sustainability & responsible management

We integrate people safety, environmental protection and regulatory compliance into every technical decision.

ESG focus

Engineering for safety and the environment

Environmental

Drainage and seepage control, closure and post-closure plans, aligned with SEIA (Law 19.300) and Chile's National Tailings Deposits Plan.

Social

We prioritize worker and community safety in the spirit of GISTM: transparency, accountability and rights protection.

Governance

Independent reviews, GISTM gap analysis and monitoring-data traceability for auditable decisions.

Safe operations

We promote PPE culture in the field and deliverables that reduce failure risk at tailings facilities.

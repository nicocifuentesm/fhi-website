About

A team built on experience

FHI was founded on the conviction that tailings engineering requires technical rigor, proven experience and an agile structure. Our team brings together professionals who have led tailings, geotechnical and monitoring projects in Chilean large-scale mining and in international operations. Each specialist contributes direct experience in the disciplines critical to tailings management.

We combine the proximity and flexibility of a boutique firm with the technical rigor and standards of global consultancies. We work under GISTM, ICMM, CDA frameworks and Chilean SERNAGEOMIN and SEIA (Law 19.300) regulations, delivering verifiable technical decisions across the facility life cycle.

Mission

Deliver engineering excellence that protects people, the environment and our clients' assets.

Team

Specialists in the critical disciplines

FHI's team brings together professionals experienced in geotechnics, hydrogeology, structural geology, civil engineering, instrumentation monitoring and environmental management.

Our specialists have contributed to projects spanning foundation and borrow-material characterization, drainage and seepage-control design, static and pseudostatic numerical stability modeling, and monitoring programs using satellite InSAR and piezometer networks — complemented by Chilean regulatory frameworks (SERNAGEOMIN, SEIA) and international standards (GISTM, ICMM, CDA).

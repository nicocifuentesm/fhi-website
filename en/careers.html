Careers at FHI

An agile firm where senior experience meets the most demanding technical challenges in Chilean mining.

Culture

Engineering with rigor and proximity

At FHI we work in dedicated teams, with direct client contact and technical decisions backed by data, Chilean regulations (SERNAGEOMIN, SEIA) and international standards (GISTM, CDA, ICMM).

We look for professionals who value field safety (PPE, procedures), deliverable precision and clear communication of complex findings.

  • Real projects — Tailings, geotechnics, monitoring & InSAR
  • Senior mentoring — Learning in the field and office
  • Agile structure — Fewer layers, more technical impact
  • Safety first — PPE culture and regulatory compliance
